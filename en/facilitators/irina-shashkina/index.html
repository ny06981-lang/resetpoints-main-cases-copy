When a retreat is useful

When a company is stuck, decisions are not producing results, and a strong team still does not operate as one system.

  • The business has developed an internal mismatch
  • People are working from different logics and cannot hear each other
  • The team needs to step out of operations and see the whole system
  • The company is entering a new phase of growth or change

Working format

Before the retreat: interviews and context gathering. During 2–3 days: the current situation, contradictions, business logic, and team dynamics. After: implementation support when needed.
