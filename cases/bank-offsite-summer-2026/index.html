Case / large bank / summer 2026

Leadership offsite: meeting as people, not only as roles

A two-day countryside offsite for 50 people from a large bank's leadership and middle management group. The goal was not a heavy strategy session, but a high-quality shared experience that restored energy, contact, and ease.

50 participants 2 days Countryside hotel
Need An offsite without a heavy agenda, but with real engagement
Format Welcome, short official part, game, BBQ, music, and free time
Effect Unity, lightness, and the feeling of meeting as people again

Context

The team needed a retreat that would not feel like another working day outside the office. We kept the agenda light and built a clear rhythm: align briefly, switch into play, spend the evening together, and leave enough space for informal contact.

For this audience, precision matters. The experience had to feel well-held, but not overproduced: easy to join, comfortable to stay in, and strong enough to become a shared memory.

Program

12:00

Arrival

Navigation, welcome food, and a soft group landing.

12:30

Official part

A short shared context without turning the day into a conference.

15:00

Team game

A light mechanic people could join without awkwardness.

17:30

Evening

Teams, BBQ, cover band, bar, tea zone, and karaoke.

Day 2

Free time

Breakfast, SPA, walks, water, and a soft departure.

Result

It was 10 out of 10.

The result was not a formal team-building checkbox, but a visible shift in the room: people mixed, laughed, stayed after the official part, and left with a warm shared memory.
